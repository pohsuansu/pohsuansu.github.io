簡介About

ABOUT

我是國立臺北護理健康大學健康科技學院的助理教授，研究重心是把人工智慧、機器學習與資料分析導入精準健康與醫療照護。目前主持國科會「結合 RAG-LLM 與語音互動的智慧運動處方 AIoT 系統」計畫，並在文化健康站與偏鄉日間照護機構推動 AI 營養與運動支援的健康促進模式。

我的訓練背景是分子生物學與癌症表觀遺傳學。2012 年於國防醫學院取得醫學科學博士後，於雙和醫院婦產部與表基因轉譯醫學中心從事研究近十年，主題包括以子宮頸抹片 DNA 甲基化偵測子宮內膜癌與卵巢癌，以及以子宮頸分泌物甲基化預測胚胎植入結果。這段經驗讓我在醫療 AI 研究中，同時重視臨床可用性與資料的生物學意義。

I am an Assistant Professor at the College of Health Technology, National Taipei University of Nursing and Health Sciences. My work brings artificial intelligence, machine learning and data analytics into precision health and healthcare delivery. I currently lead a National Science and Technology Council (NSTC) project on a RAG-LLM, voice-interactive AIoT system for personalised exercise prescription, and run AI-supported nutrition and physical-activity programmes at Indigenous cultural health stations and rural day-care centres.

My training is in molecular biology and cancer epigenetics. After a PhD in Medical Sciences at the National Defense Medical Center (2012), I spent close to ten years at Shuang Ho Hospital developing DNA methylation biomarkers — detecting endometrial and ovarian cancer from cervical scrapings and predicting embryo-implantation outcomes from cervical secretions. That background shapes how I approach medical AI: models must be clinically usable and biologically interpretable.

研究主題Research themes

RESEARCH
01

生成式 AI 與 AIoT 健康促進Generative AI & AIoT for health promotion

以檢索增強生成（retrieval-augmented generation, RAG）結合大型語言模型，搭配語音互動與穿戴裝置，為中高齡與慢性病族群提供個人化運動處方與營養建議，並在實際照護場域驗證可行性。

Retrieval-augmented large language models combined with voice interaction and wearable devices to deliver personalised exercise prescriptions and nutrition advice for older adults and people with chronic disease, evaluated in real care settings.

RAG-LLM · AIoT · 運動處方 · AI 營養師exercise prescription · AI dietitian
02

原住民族與偏鄉高齡健康Indigenous & rural aging health

於文化健康站與偏鄉日間照護機構導入 AI 營養與運動支援模組，結合規律體適能活動，建構長者整合型健康促進模式。

Integrating AI nutrition and physical-activity modules with regular fitness programmes at Indigenous cultural health stations and rural day-care centres to build an integrated health-promotion model for older adults.

原住民族健康資料的蒐集與 AI 應用，以文化安全與部落資料治理為前提，不只從技術面處理。Data collection and AI use with Indigenous communities are grounded in cultural safety and Indigenous data governance, not treated as a purely technical problem.
03

臨床預測模型與醫療 AIClinical prediction & medical AI

以隨機存活森林等可解釋模型建立肝細胞癌術後預後預測、以機器學習分析胃癌病理切片，並應用於長照個案管理與公共衛生資料整合，作為臨床決策與政策規劃的基礎。

Interpretable models such as random survival forests for post-operative prognosis in hepatocellular carcinoma, machine learning for gastric-cancer pathology slides, and AI support for long-term-care case management and public-health data integration.

存活分析 · 可解釋 AI · 數位病理 · 健康大數據survival analysis · explainable AI · digital pathology · health big data
04

表觀遺傳生物標記Epigenetic biomarkers

以 DNA 甲基化建立非侵入性檢測：從子宮頸抹片偵測子宮內膜癌與卵巢癌、從子宮頸分泌物預測冷凍胚胎植入的活產結果，並探討 TET1、PAX1、NKX6-1 等基因在癌症幹細胞中的角色。

Non-invasive DNA methylation assays — detecting endometrial and ovarian cancer from cervical scrapings and predicting live birth after frozen embryo transfer from cervical secretions — and the roles of TET1, PAX1 and NKX6-1 in cancer stemness.

DNA 甲基化 · 婦癌篩檢 · 生殖醫學 · 生物資訊DNA methylation · gynaecological cancer screening · reproductive medicine · bioinformatics
